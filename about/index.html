About MGTECHNZ

Founded over 10 years ago, MGTECHNZ is a 100% New Zealand owned and operated technology provider based in the Waikato region.

We specialise in delivering secure, scalable, and modern IT solutions — from cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity to custom software and managed support — tailored specifically for Kiwi businesses.

Our team combines local knowledge with global expertise, ensuring every client gets reliable, compliant, and future-proof technology that helps them grow.

We are proud to have served over 200 businesses across New Zealand, and we remain committed to excellence, security, and 24/7 support you can trust.

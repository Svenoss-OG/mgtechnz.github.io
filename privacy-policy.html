Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 | Compliant with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020

1. Who We Are

MGTECHNZ (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a technology services provider based in New Zealand. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information, in compliance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number
  • Information you provide via enquiry forms or email
  • Website usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited (no personal identification)

3. How We Use Your Information

We only use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide requested services
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • Comply with legal obligations under New Zealand law

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share it when required by law, or with trusted service providers who assist us — always under strict confidentiality agreements and data protection standards.

5. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, aligned with NZ Privacy Act requirements and industry best practices.

6. Your Rights

Under NZ law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the information we hold about you
  • Ask for corrections if information is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Request deletion where legally permitted

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us at: info@mgtechnz.com
